Beginning

One day, my manager told me to go to the company next to ours for work. I sat there crying because I really didn’t want to go there. The moment I arrived, I heard a man speaking loudly, and it scared me.

After that, I never felt safe or comfortable unless I was with him. And the day I cried because I had to go to that company turned out to be the most beautiful day of my life. ❤️

Honestly, I didn’t know that fate would bring me to him and that the fear I felt would turn into comfort, warmth, and something I’d never want to lose.
